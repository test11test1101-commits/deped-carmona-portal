DepEd Vision, Mission, Core Values & Mandate

👁️ The DepEd Vision

"We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

As a learner-centered public institution, the Department of Education continuously improves itself to better serve its stakeholders."

🎯 The DepEd Mission

To protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education where:

  • Students learn in a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating environment.
  • Teachers facilitate learning and constantly nurture every learner.
  • Administrators and staff, as stewards of the institution, ensure an enabling and supportive environment for effective learning to happen.
  • Family, community, and other stakeholders are actively engaged and share responsibility for developing life-long learners.

SDO Carmona City History & Milestones

City Schools Division of Carmona

Department of Education • Region IV-A CALABARZON

Carmona Educational Cluster (Pre-Cityhood)

Carmona schools originally operated with distinction under the Schools Division Office of Cavite Province, establishing renowned academic benchmarks, athletic championships, and strong community-school linkages across elementary and secondary clusters.

Ratification of Republic Act No. 11938 (July 8, 2023)

With overwhelming democratic support in the plebiscite, Carmona was formally converted into a component city of Cavite. This historic elevation laid the legal and operational foundation for an autonomous City Schools Division.

Creation of the City Schools Division of Carmona

The establishment of SDO Carmona under DepEd CALABARZON brought direct governance, localized curriculum enhancements, dedicated financial stewardship, and fast frontline services to all public elementary and secondary schools in the City of Carmona.

Present: Transforming Basic Education under MATATAG

Today, SDO Carmona champions transparent governance, digital record tracking, learning recovery programs, and faculty excellence celebrated annually through the Gawad Dalub-Kandili awards.

Data Privacy Notice & Protection Policy

Commitment to Republic Act No. 10173

The City Schools Division of Carmona adheres strictly to the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) and National Privacy Commission guidelines to safeguard all personal and sensitive information entrusted to our care.

1. Information We Collect

Learner information (LIS data, enrollment forms, academic credentials), personnel service records, visitor logs, and public feedback submissions through the Client Satisfaction Measurement (CSM) system.

2. Purpose of Processing

Data is processed solely for educational administration, verification of official scholastic records, personnel management, statistical reporting to DepEd Regional and Central Offices, and addressing public inquiries.

3. Data Security & Storage

Physical documents and digital databases are secured under strict multi-factor access protocols, encryption, and institutional safeguards. No personal data is shared with third parties without authorized statutory consent.

4. Data Subject Rights & Contact

You have the right to request access to, rectify, or lodge inquiries regarding your personal information. Reach our Division Data Protection Officer at: carmonacity@deped.gov.ph.
