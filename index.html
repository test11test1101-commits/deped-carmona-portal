Website Accessibility Statement

Compliance with Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1)

The City Schools Division of Carmona web portal is designed and maintained in strict adherence to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1 Level AA) and ISO/IEC 40500 standards for public electronic services.

Key Accessibility Features:
  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements, menus, forms, and dialogs are navigable using standard keyboard focus (Tab, Enter, Space, Escape).
  • High Contrast Mode: One-click high-contrast mode conforming to AAA contrast ratios.
  • Font Scalability: Dynamic resizing controls allowing scalable typography without breaking layout structure.
  • Dyslexia Friendly Font: Alternates typography to high-legibility sans-serif spacing.

Citizen's Charter & Frontline Services

In accordance with Republic Act No. 11032 (Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018), here are the standard processing times for frontline services of SDO Carmona:

Issuance of Requested Official Documents / Certifications (Records)
Processing Time: 1 to 3 Working Days • Fee: None
Processing of Application for Leave of Absence (Personnel)
Processing Time: 1 to 2 Working Days • Fee: None
Review and Approval of Travel Authority (OSDS)
Processing Time: 2 Working Days • Fee: None
Evaluation and Processing of School MOOE Cash Advances (Accounting)
Processing Time: 3 to 5 Working Days • Fee: None
